How ordering works

No account, no card form. You order and pay through a conversation with us.

Delivery and common questions

Can I buy by the yard?

Yes. Fabrics are sold per yard or per full piece, and we cut to your length.

Do you offer wholesale prices?

Yes. Message us the items and quantity and we'll send a wholesale quote.

Can I see the fabric before paying?

Ask for a free swatch on WhatsApp. For large orders we can arrange a visit to our store.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer after we confirm your order on WhatsApp. Card payment can be added later.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us the project and we'll recommend fabric, quantity and price.
