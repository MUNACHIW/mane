About Mane Textiles

Mane Textiles began as a single stall selling bedsheets and lace. Today we supply homes, hotels, event planners and tailors across Nigeria.

We source directly from mills and weavers so our prices stay fair and our quality stays consistent. Every roll is measured and checked before it leaves Lagos.

Whether you need two yards for a shirt or two hundred for a hotel fit-out, we cut to your length and ship to your door.
